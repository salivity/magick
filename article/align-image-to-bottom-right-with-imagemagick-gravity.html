Align Image to Bottom-Right with ImageMagick Gravity

ImageMagick provides the -gravity operator to establish reference points for image placement, text annotation, and cropping operations. In ImageMagick's coordinate system, compass directions define anchor points, meaning the Southeast setting is used to align content directly to the bottom-right corner. This guide explains how to use the -gravity Southeast option across common image manipulation tasks including overlaying watermarks, cropping, and rendering text.

The -gravity Southeast Operator

ImageMagick treats the canvas as a compass grid. Setting -gravity Southeast shifts the origin (0,0) from the default top-left corner to the bottom-right corner of the canvas. Any subsequent offset values +X+Y will measure inward from the right (X) and bottom (Y) edges.

1. Overlaying an Image or Watermark

To place a smaller image, logo, or watermark in the bottom-right corner of a base image, use -gravity Southeast before the -composite operation:

magick input.jpg watermark.png -gravity Southeast -composite output.jpg

To add padding so the overlay does not touch the exact image borders, use geometry offsets:

magick input.jpg watermark.png -gravity Southeast -geometry +20+20 -composite output.jpg

In this example, +20+20 shifts the overlay 20 pixels to the left and 20 pixels up from the bottom-right corner.

2. Cropping from the Bottom-Right Corner

To crop a specific section from the bottom-right of an image, define the crop dimensions with -gravity Southeast:

magick input.jpg -gravity Southeast -crop 500x500+0+0 +repage output.jpg
  • 500x500: The width and height of the cropped area.
  • +0+0: Zero offset from the bottom-right corner.
  • +repage: Removes the canvas metadata and resets the image boundaries.

3. Adding Text to the Bottom-Right

To annotate an image with text anchored to the bottom-right:

magick input.jpg -gravity Southeast -fill white -pointsize 24 -annotate +15+15 "Copyright 2024" output.jpg

The -annotate +15+15 flag ensures the text maintains a 15-pixel margin from both the bottom and right edges of the canvas.
