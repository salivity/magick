Apply Swirl Distortion with ImageMagick

ImageMagick allows you to twist the center of an image into a spiral effect using the built-in -swirl operator. This guide explains how to use the command, adjust the intensity and direction of the distortion, and apply the effect to specific areas of an image.

Basic Syntax

To apply a swirl distortion, supply the -swirl option followed by the number of degrees you want the center pixels to rotate:

ImageMagick v7:

magick input.jpg -swirl 180 output.jpg

ImageMagick v6:

convert input.jpg -swirl 180 output.jpg

Controlling Swirl Intensity and Direction

The integer passed to -swirl represents the degrees of rotation at the exact center of the image. The effect is strongest at the center and diminishes toward the edges.

  • Clockwise Swirl: Use a positive degree value. 
    magick input.jpg -swirl 360 output.jpg
  • Counter-Clockwise Swirl: Use a negative degree value. 
    magick input.jpg -swirl -360 output.jpg
  • Extreme Swirls: Values higher than 360 (such as 720 or 1080) create tighter, multi-rotation spirals.

Swirling an Off-Center Area

By default, the swirl effect centers on the entire image. To apply the swirl to a specific region instead of the image center, use the -region operator:

magick input.jpg -region 300x300+100+100 -swirl 180 +region output.jpg
  • 300x300 sets the width and height of the target area.
  • +100+100 sets the X and Y offset for the top-left corner of the region.
  • +region removes the region lock so subsequent operations affect the full image.
