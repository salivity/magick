Built-in Canvas Patterns in ImageMagick

ImageMagick includes a wide variety of built-in patterns and textured canvases that can be generated dynamically without needing external image files. These patterns are accessed primarily through the pattern: protocol or through dedicated pseudo-image generators like granite: and plasma:. This guide outlines the available built-in canvas patterns in ImageMagick and demonstrates how to generate them.

How to Use Built-in Patterns

To generate an image using a built-in pattern, define the canvas size with the -size option and specify the pattern name as the input:

magick -size 300x300 pattern:checkerboard output.png

For built-in texture generators like granite or plasma, specify the generator name directly:

magick -size 300x300 granite: granite_output.png

Geometric and Tiling Patterns

These patterns generate repeating geometric tiles and shapes:

  • pattern:checkerboard – Alternating black and white square grid.
  • pattern:bricks – Standard rectangular brick layout.
  • pattern:hexagons – Repeating honeycomb grid of hexagons.
  • pattern:octagons – Grid of octagons.
  • pattern:circles – Repeating circular rings.
  • pattern:fishscales – Overlapping curved scale pattern.
  • pattern:smallfishscales – Tighter, smaller version of the fish scale pattern.
  • pattern:leftshingle – Angled overlapping shingle pattern facing left.
  • pattern:rightshingle – Angled overlapping shingle pattern facing right.

Line and Hatching Patterns

These patterns produce lines, stripes, and crosshatches at various angles:

  • pattern:horizontal – 1-pixel horizontal lines.
  • pattern:horizontal2 – 2-pixel spaced horizontal lines.
  • pattern:horizontal3 – 3-pixel spaced horizontal lines.
  • pattern:vertical – 1-pixel vertical lines.
  • pattern:vertical2 – 2-pixel spaced vertical lines.
  • pattern:vertical3 – 3-pixel spaced vertical lines.
  • pattern:crosshatch – Standard 90-degree intersecting grid.
  • pattern:crosshatch30 – Intersecting lines at 30-degree angles.
  • pattern:crosshatch45 – Diagonal intersecting crosshatch at 45 degrees.
  • pattern:left30 / pattern:right30 – Single-direction diagonal lines at 30 degrees.
  • pattern:left45 / pattern:right45 – Single-direction diagonal lines at 45 degrees.
  • pattern:horizontalsaw – Zigzag horizontal sawtooth lines.
  • pattern:verticalsaw – Zigzag vertical sawtooth lines.

Standard Hatch Style (HS) Patterns

ImageMagick also supports standard Hatch Style (HS) fill patterns:

  • pattern:hs_horizontal – Standard horizontal hatch.
  • pattern:hs_vertical – Standard vertical hatch.
  • pattern:hs_fdiag – Forward diagonal hatch ().
  • pattern:hs_bdiag – Backward diagonal hatch (/).
  • pattern:hs_cross – Cross hatch (+).
  • pattern:hs_diagcross – Diagonal cross hatch (X).

Grayscale Dot Screen Patterns

These patterns provide dithered dot grids representing percentage gray levels:

  • pattern:gray10 through pattern:gray90 – Halftone dot screens ranging from 10% to 90% gray density in 5% and 10% increments.

Built-in Texture and Dynamic Canvases

In addition to tiled patterns, ImageMagick includes non-repeating procedural canvas textures:

  • granite: – Generates a grayscale, mineral-like granite noise texture.
  • plasma: – Generates a multi-colored fractal plasma cloud (can be customized, e.g., plasma:fractal or plasma:red-blue).
  • gradient: / radial-gradient: – Generates smooth linear and radial transitions between colors.
  • canvas:color / xc:color – Generates a solid color canvas (e.g., canvas:white or xc:transparent).
