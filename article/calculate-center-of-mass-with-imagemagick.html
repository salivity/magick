Calculate Center of Mass with ImageMagick

Calculating the center of mass (or centroid) of an isolated object is a standard computer vision task used to determine the balance point or positional anchor of a shape. This guide explains how to preprocess an image to isolate a target object and use ImageMagick's built-in moment analysis tools to calculate and extract its precise center of mass coordinates.

Step 1: Preprocess the Image into a Binary Mask

The center of mass is calculated based on pixel intensity weights. To calculate the geometric centroid of an isolated object, the object should be represented as solid white (intensity value 1) against a solid black background (intensity value 0).

Run the following command to convert the image to grayscale and apply a threshold:

magick input.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 50% mask.png

Note: If the target object is dark on a light background, add the -negate flag to invert the colors so the object becomes white.

magick input.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 50% -negate mask.png

Step 2: Compute Moments and Extract Centroid Coordinates

ImageMagick provides the -moments operator, which calculates raw and normalized image moments. The centroid coordinates \((X_c, Y_c)\) correspond to the ratio of the first-order spatial moments to the zero-order moment (\(M_{10}/M_{00}\) and \(M_{01}/M_{00}\)).

To display the computed moments for the image, run:

magick mask.png -moments -identify null:

Look for the Centroid: line under the channel moment statistics in the output:

Image moments:
  Channel 0:
    Centroid: 142.35, 98.62

In this example, the center of mass for the object is located at \(X = 142.35\) and \(Y = 98.62\).

Step 3: Run as a Single-Line Pipeline

You can combine the preprocessing and coordinate extraction into a single command without creating intermediate files.

For ImageMagick 7 on Unix-like systems (Linux/macOS):

magick input.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 50% -moments info: | grep -m 1 "Centroid:"

For ImageMagick 6:

convert input.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 50% -moments -verbose info: | grep -m 1 "Centroid:"

Step 4: Verify the Coordinates Visually

To verify the calculated center of mass, draw a marker (such as a small colored circle) at the coordinates on the original image:

magick input.png -fill red -stroke red -draw "circle 142.35,98.62 142.35,101.62" verified.png
