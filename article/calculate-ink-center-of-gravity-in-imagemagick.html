Calculate Ink Center of Gravity in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to compute the center of gravity (centroid) of ink distribution across a printed or scanned page using ImageMagick. By converting the document to grayscale, negating the colors so ink represents mass, and calculating image spatial moments, you can quickly extract the exact X and Y coordinates representing the physical center of the ink distribution.

The Underlying Concept

Standard spatial image moments calculate the centroid based on pixel luminance, where white pixels (value 1) represent mass and black pixels (value 0) represent empty space. Because ink is typically dark on a light background, the page must first be inverted so the ink holds the positive mass values required for correct calculation.

The Command-Line Procedure

Using ImageMagick version 7, execute the following command in your terminal:

magick input_page.png -colorspace Gray -negate -moments -format "Centroid X: %[moments:centroid.x]\nCentroid Y: %[moments:centroid.y]\n" info:

Step-by-Step Breakdown

  1. -colorspace Gray: Converts the document to a single grayscale channel to eliminate any color variations and streamline computation.
  2. -negate: Inverts the pixel values. The white page background turns to black (zero intensity), and the dark ink turns into varying shades of light or pure white (positive intensity).
  3. -moments: Instructs ImageMagick to compute the first- and second-order image moments, including spatial centroids and Hu invariants.
  4. -format "%[moments:centroid.x] %[moments:centroid.y]": Extracts the horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) coordinates of the calculated centroid directly to standard output.
  5. info:: Outputs the metadata without saving a new image file to disk.

Handling Scanned Noise and Background Tint

Scanned documents often feature off-white backgrounds, shadows, or bleed-through from reverse pages, which can skew the center of gravity calculation. To isolate only the actual text or ink markings, apply a threshold:

magick input_page.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 80% -negate -moments -format "Centroid X: %[moments:centroid.x]\nCentroid Y: %[moments:centroid.y]\n" info:

The -threshold 80% argument forces near-white background noise completely to white before the -negate step, ensuring only definitive ink strokes contribute to the final center of mass.

Interpreting the Output

The resulting values represent pixel coordinates relative to the standard ImageMagick origin (0, 0), located at the top-left corner of the image:

  • Centroid X: The horizontal distance in pixels from the left border to the center of the ink.
  • Centroid Y: The vertical distance in pixels from the top border to the center of the ink.
