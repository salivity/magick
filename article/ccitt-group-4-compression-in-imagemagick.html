CCITT Group 4 Compression in ImageMagick

CCITT Group 4 is a lossless compression algorithm designed specifically for 1-bit monochrome (bi-level black and white) images, making it the industry standard for archiving scanned text documents and line art. This guide explains how to convert scanned files into highly compressed, lightweight TIFF or PDF documents using ImageMagick's CCITT Group 4 compression.

Prerequisites for CCITT Group 4

CCITT Group 4 (also known as Group 4 Fax or CCITT T.6) requires the input image to be strictly 1-bit monochrome. It cannot process grayscale or color images directly without converting them to pure black and white first.

Basic Command Structure

To compress an image using CCITT Group 4 in ImageMagick, use the -compress Group4 option along with a method to convert the image to 1-bit monochrome:

magick input.png -monochrome -compress Group4 output.tif

Step-by-Step Conversion and Optimization

1. Converting to Pure Monochrome (Thresholding)

While -monochrome uses dithering, scanned documents usually look cleaner using thresholding. Thresholding converts all pixels darker than a specific value to black and all lighter pixels to white.

magick scan.jpg -threshold 60% -compress Group4 output.tif
  • -threshold 60%: Any pixel with luminance below 60% becomes black, and above 60% becomes white. Adjust this percentage depending on the brightness and contrast of the scan.

2. Creating CCITT Group 4 Compressed PDFs

You can apply Group 4 compression directly to single or multi-page PDF files:

magick scan.png -threshold 50% -compress Group4 output.pdf

To control the physical print size and clarity, specify the density (DPI):

magick -density 300 scan.png -threshold 50% -compress Group4 output.pdf

3. Processing Multi-Page Documents

To combine multiple scanned pages into a single CCITT Group 4 compressed TIFF or PDF:

magick -density 300 page1.png page2.png page3.png -threshold 55% -compress Group4 document.pdf

Or for a multi-page TIFF:

magick page*.png -threshold 55% -compress Group4 archive.tif

Essential Command Options Reference

  • -compress Group4: Applies CCITT Group 4 compression to the output file.
  • -threshold [value]%: Converts color or grayscale to pure black and white without dithering artifacts.
  • -monochrome: Converts the image to 1-bit bi-level using Floyd-Steinberg dithering.
  • -density [DPI]: Sets the horizontal and vertical resolution metadata (e.g., 300 DPI), ensuring proper sizing in PDF viewers and print output.
  • -type Bilevel: Explicitly forces the output image type to 1-bit black and white.
