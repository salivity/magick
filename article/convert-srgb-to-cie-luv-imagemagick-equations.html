Convert sRGB to CIE Luv: ImageMagick Equations

This article details the mathematical pipeline used by ImageMagick to transform pixel buffers from the non-linear sRGB colorspace to the perceptually uniform CIE \(L^*u^*v^*\) (CIELUV) colorspace. The transformation is executed in a multi-step process: first, non-linear sRGB values are linearized by removing the gamma curve; next, the linear RGB components are projected onto the standard CIE XYZ space via a matrix transformation calibrated for a standard D65 illuminant; finally, the CIE XYZ coordinates are mapped to the \(L^*\), \(u^*\), and \(v^*\) coordinates.

1. sRGB De-quantization and Linearization

ImageMagick normalizes raw 8-bit or 16-bit integer pixel buffer channels to floating-point values in the range \([0.0, 1.0]\). Because standard sRGB channels (\(R_{\text{srgb}}, G_{\text{srgb}}, B_{\text{srgb}}\)) use non-linear gamma companding, each channel \(C_{\text{srgb}}\) must be linearized to \(C_{\text{linear}}\):

\[\begin{aligned} C_{\text{linear}} = \begin{cases} \frac{C_{\text{srgb}}}{12.92}, & \text{if } C_{\text{srgb}} \le 0.04045 \\ \left(\frac{C_{\text{srgb}} + 0.055}{1.055}\right)^{2.4}, & \text{if } C_{\text{srgb}} > 0.04045 \end{cases} \end{aligned}\]

This conversion is applied independently to the Red, Green, and Blue channels.

2. Linear RGB to CIE 1931 XYZ

The linearized channels (\(R_{\text{linear}}, G_{\text{linear}}, B_{\text{linear}}\)) are converted to CIE XYZ tristimulus values using the standard sRGB transformation matrix under the CIE standard illuminant D65 reference white point:

$$\begin{bmatrix} X \ Y \ Z \end{bmatrix}

\begin{bmatrix} 0.4124564 & 0.3575761 & 0.1804375 \ 0.2126729 & 0.7151522 & 0.0721750 \ 0.0193339 & 0.1191920 & 0.9503041 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} R_{\text{linear}} \ G_{\text{linear}} \ B_{\text{linear}} \end{bmatrix}$$

3. CIE XYZ to CIE Luv*

ImageMagick maps the resulting \(X, Y, Z\) values to the 1976 CIE \(L^*u^*v^*\) space using the D65 white point coordinates (\(X_r, Y_r, Z_r\)):

\[X_r = 0.95047, \quad Y_r = 1.00000, \quad Z_r = 1.08883\]

Chromaticity Coordinates

The intermediate chromaticity coordinates \((u', v')\) of the sample color and \((u'_r, v'_r)\) of the reference white are calculated:

\[u' = \frac{4X}{X + 15Y + 3Z}, \quad v' = \frac{9Y}{X + 15Y + 3Z}\]

\[u'_r = \frac{4X_r}{X_r + 15Y_r + 3Z_r} \approx 0.19783001\]

\[v'_r = \frac{9Y_r}{X_r + 15Y_r + 3Z_r} \approx 0.46831999\]

If \(X = Y = Z = 0\), \(u'\) and \(v'\) are defined as \(0\).

Lightness (\(L^*\))

The lightness component \(L^*\) is computed based on the ratio \(y_r = \frac{Y}{Y_r} = Y\):

\[L^* = \begin{cases} 116 \cdot (y_r)^{1/3} - 16, & \text{if } y_r > \left(\frac{6}{29}\right)^3 \ (\approx 0.008856) \\ \left(\frac{29}{3}\right)^3 \cdot y_r, & \text{if } y_r \le \left(\frac{6}{29}\right)^3 \ (\approx 903.3 \cdot y_r) \end{cases}\]

Chromatic Components (\(u^*\) and \(v^*\))

The chromaticity coordinates \(u^*\) and \(v^*\) are scaled by the calculated lightness \(L^*\):

\[u^* = 13 \cdot L^* \cdot (u' - u'_r)\]

\[v^* = 13 \cdot L^* \cdot (v' - v'_r)\]

The resulting tuple \((L^*, u^*, v^*)\) represents the final pixel values stored or processed within ImageMagick's CIELUV buffer.
