Create Oil Painting Effects in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to convert standard digital photographs into simulated oil paintings using ImageMagick command-line tools. You will learn how to use the built-in -paint operator, how the radius parameter directly dictates the simulated brush size, and how to combine secondary adjustments like contrast and sharpness to produce a convincing traditional media aesthetic.

The Core Operator: -paint

ImageMagick provides a dedicated operator called -paint to simulate the look of an oil painting. The operator works by analyzing a circular neighborhood around each pixel and replacing that pixel's color with the most frequently occurring color within that radius.

The parameter supplied to -paint determines the radius of this neighborhood in pixels, which acts as the brush stroke size:

magick input.jpg -paint <radius> output.jpg

Note: In ImageMagick version 6, replace magick with convert.

Controlling the Brush Size

The size of the brush stroke is entirely controlled by the numeric argument passed to -paint.

1. Fine Detail (Small Brush)

A smaller radius retains finer details, edge definitions, and textures, resembling a painting done with fine-tipped brushes.

magick input.jpg -paint 2 fine_brush.jpg

2. Medium Detail (Standard Brush)

A radius between 4 and 8 provides a balanced painterly appearance suitable for standard-resolution portraits and landscapes.

magick input.jpg -paint 5 medium_brush.jpg

3. Broad Strokes (Large Brush)

Higher values blend large areas of color together, producing an abstract, heavy-impasto effect.

magick input.jpg -paint 12 broad_brush.jpg

Scaling Brush Size by Image Resolution

Because the radius is measured in absolute pixels, the visual size of the brush depends on the input image's resolution. A radius of 4 on a 600x400 image creates large strokes, whereas the same radius on a 4000x3000 image is barely noticeable.

For high-resolution images, use larger radius values (e.g., 10 to 25) to achieve visible brushwork, or downscale the image prior to processing:

magick input.jpg -resize 1920x1080 -paint 6 output.jpg

Enhancing the Painterly Aesthetic

Standard oil paintings feature rich color saturation, defined shadows, and surface texture. Combining -paint with additional ImageMagick operators produces a more convincing result:

magick input.jpg \
  -modulate 105,125,100 \
  -paint 6 \
  -unsharp 0x1.5+1.0+0 \
  output_enhanced.jpg
  • -modulate 105,125,100: Increases brightness by 5% and color saturation by 25% to mimic oil pigments.
  • -paint 6: Applies the medium brush oil painting effect.
  • -unsharp 0x1.5+1.0+0: Restores sharpness to stroke edges to simulate the crisp ridges left by a physical paintbrush.
