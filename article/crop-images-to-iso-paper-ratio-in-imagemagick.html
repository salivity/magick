Crop Images to ISO Paper Ratio in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to automatically crop any image to standard ISO 216 paper ratios (such as A4, A3, or A5) using ImageMagick. Standard ISO paper sizes share a fixed aspect ratio of \(1:\sqrt{2}\) (approximately \(1:1.4142\)). Below, you will find the exact commands required to center-crop images to this ratio dynamically for both portrait and landscape orientations without manual dimension calculations.

Understanding the ISO Aspect Ratio

All standard A-series paper formats adhere to the same proportions:

  • Portrait: \(1 : 1.4142\) (or \(1 : \sqrt{2}\))
  • Landscape: \(1.4142 : 1\) (or \(\sqrt{2} : 1\))

To fit an image to an ISO page without distortion, ImageMagick must calculate the largest possible bounding box matching this ratio, center it, and trim the excess pixels.

Crop to Portrait ISO Ratio (ImageMagick 7)

To crop an image to a vertical ISO paper proportion while keeping the maximum possible area centered, run:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop "%[fx:w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h/sqrt(2) : w]x%[fx:w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h : w*sqrt(2)]+0+0" +repage output_portrait.jpg

How it works:

  • -gravity center: Ensures the crop area is centered horizontally and vertically.
  • fx: expressions: Compares the current aspect ratio with \(1/\sqrt{2}\). If the image is wider than the target ratio, it crops the sides; if it is taller, it crops the top and bottom.
  • +repage: Removes the virtual canvas offsets left over from the crop operation.

Crop to Landscape ISO Ratio (ImageMagick 7)

To crop an image to a horizontal ISO paper proportion:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop "%[fx:w/h > sqrt(2) ? h*sqrt(2) : w]x%[fx:w/h > sqrt(2) ? h : w/sqrt(2)]+0+0" +repage output_landscape.jpg

Auto-Detecting Orientation (Smart ISO Crop)

If you have mixed portrait and landscape images and want each cropped to the nearest ISO orientation matching its native layout:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop "%[fx:w>=h ? (w/h > sqrt(2) ? h*sqrt(2) : w) : (w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h/sqrt(2) : w)]x%[fx:w>=h ? (w/h > sqrt(2) ? h : w/sqrt(2)) : (w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h : w*sqrt(2))]+0+0" +repage output_auto.jpg

Cropping and Resizing to Exact Print Sizes (e.g., A4 at 300 DPI)

An A4 page at 300 DPI measures exactly 2480 × 3508 pixels. You can crop and scale to this exact resolution in a single step using the ^ fill modifier and -crop:

# Portrait A4 at 300 DPI
magick input.jpg -resize "2480x3508^" -gravity center -crop 2480x3508+0+0 +repage output_a4.jpg

# Landscape A4 at 300 DPI
magick input.jpg -resize "3508x2480^" -gravity center -crop 3508x2480+0+0 +repage output_a4_landscape.jpg

Batch Processing Multiple Images

To process an entire folder of .jpg images and save the cropped versions to an output directory:

mkdir -p output
magick mogrify -path output -gravity center -crop "%[fx:w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h/sqrt(2) : w]x%[fx:w/h > 1/sqrt(2) ? h : w*sqrt(2)]+0+0" +repage *.jpg
