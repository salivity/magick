Crop Scans to ISO Paper Sizes in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to crop scanned document images to match standard ISO 216 paper dimensions (such as A4, A3, or A5) using ImageMagick command-line tools. You will learn how to crop based on fixed scan resolutions (DPI) as well as how to enforce the standard ISO aspect ratio (\(1:\sqrt{2}\)) dynamically across portrait and landscape orientations.

Understanding ISO Aspect Ratios and Dimensions

All standard ISO 216 paper sizes (A series) share a fixed aspect ratio of \(1 : \sqrt{2}\), which is approximately 1 : 1.4142 (or 1.4142 : 1 for landscape).

When cropping scans by absolute pixel dimensions, standard sizes at 300 DPI are:

  • A4: 2480 × 3508 pixels
  • A3: 3508 × 4960 pixels
  • A5: 1748 × 2480 pixels

Command 1: Crop by Exact Pixel Dimensions (Recommended for Known DPI)

If your document was scanned at a fixed resolution (such as 300 DPI), you can center-crop the image to standard A4 dimensions with:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop 2480x3508+0+0 +repage output_a4.jpg

Options Breakdown:

  • -gravity center: Ensures the crop box is centered, trimming equal amounts from opposite borders.
  • -crop 2480x3508+0+0: Specifies the width, height, and offset (0, 0) for the target standard dimension.
  • +repage: Removes the embedded virtual canvas metadata to ensure the output image file displays the correct final canvas size.

Command 2: Dynamic ISO Aspect Ratio Crop (Resolution Independent)

If you have scans of arbitrary resolutions and want to enforce the exact ISO \(1:\sqrt{2}\) aspect ratio without scaling the pixels, use ImageMagick's FX expressions to calculate the cropping box dynamically:

For Portrait Orientation:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop "%[fx: (w/h > 1/1.4142) ? (h/1.4142) : w]x%[fx: (w/h > 1/1.4142) ? h : (w*1.4142)]+0+0" +repage output.jpg

For Landscape Orientation:

magick input.jpg -gravity center -crop "%[fx: (w/h > 1.4142) ? (h*1.4142) : w]x%[fx: (w/h > 1.4142) ? h : (w/1.4142)]+0+0" +repage output.jpg

Command 3: Automatic Border Removal and Standard Cropping

Scanned documents often include black borders, shadows, or scanner margins. You can combine automated trimming with an exact ISO crop in a single command:

magick input.jpg -fuzz 10% -trim +repage -gravity center -crop 2480x3508+0+0 +repage output_a4.jpg
  • -fuzz 10%: Treats colors within a 10% margin of the corner pixels as background noise.
  • -trim: Removes outer margins before the document is cropped to the exact ISO size.
