Enhance Faint Handwriting Using ImageMagick

This guide explains how to restore and enhance faint pencil handwriting from scanned documents using ImageMagick. Scanned pencil marks often suffer from low contrast, thin strokes, and uneven paper backgrounds. By applying techniques such as background normalization, level adjustments, morphological dilation, and selective sharpening, you can significantly improve legibility and make faint text dark, crisp, and readable.

1. Normalize Uneven Backgrounds

Pencil scans frequently have paper texture, shadows, or yellowed backgrounds that obscure light graphite marks. Dividing the image by a heavily blurred version of itself removes uneven lighting while preserving the text.

magick input.png -colorspace gray \( +clone -blur 0x25 \) -compose Divide -composite background_normalized.png
  • -colorspace gray: Converts the image to grayscale, removing paper discoloration.
  • \( +clone -blur 0x25 \): Creates a blurred copy representing the paper's lighting variations.
  • -compose Divide -composite: Divides the original by the background, creating a uniform, clean white background.

2. Darken and Boost Contrast

Once the background is even, you can darken the faint gray pencil marks using level adjustments or linear stretching.

magick background_normalized.png -level 30%,85% contrast_enhanced.png
  • -level 30%,85%: Forces the darkest 30% of pixels to pure black and the lightest 15% to pure white, significantly darkening faint strokes while clearing remaining gray haze. Adjust these percentages according to how faint the pencil is.

Alternatively, use automatic stretching if you have variable documents:

magick background_normalized.png -linear-stretch 2%x2% contrast_enhanced.png

3. Thicken Thin or Broken Strokes

Graphite often produces broken or excessively thin lines. Morphological dilation expands dark pixels to make strokes thicker and more solid.

magick contrast_enhanced.png -negate -morphology Dilate Diamond:1 -negate thickened.png
  • -negate: Inverts the image so text becomes white, which is required for ImageMagick's morphological dilation.
  • -morphology Dilate Diamond:1: Expands the edges of the text outward by one pixel.
  • -negate: Inverts the image back to dark text on a light background.

4. Sharpen Stroke Edges

Adding an unsharp mask sharpens the boundaries of the pencil lines, making handwriting appear crisper.

magick thickened.png -unsharp 0x1+1.5+0.05 sharpened.png
  • -unsharp 0x1+1.5+0.05: Applies an unsharp filter (radius 0, sigma 1, amount 1.5, threshold 0.05) to sharpen line contours without introducing excessive noise.

5. All-in-One Command

To perform the complete enhancement in a single step, combine the operations into one command:

magick input.png -colorspace gray \
  \( +clone -blur 0x25 \) -compose Divide -composite \
  -linear-stretch 2%x5% \
  -negate -morphology Dilate Diamond:1 -negate \
  -unsharp 0x1+1.0+0.05 \
  enhanced_output.png

Adjust the -blur radius depending on the scan resolution (use 0x50 for 300+ DPI scans) and tweak the -linear-stretch parameters to balance stroke darkness against background cleanliness.
