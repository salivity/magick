Extract Horizontal Lines with ImageMagick

This guide demonstrates how to extract horizontal ruling lines from scanned forms using ImageMagick without capturing or destroying intersecting text. By applying morphological operations with a custom directional kernel, you can isolate continuous horizontal rules based purely on geometry, filtering out letters, serifs, and vertical boundaries.

The Command

To isolate horizontal lines from a black-and-white scanned document, run the following ImageMagick command:

magick scanned_form.png -threshold 60% -negate -morphology Open "Rectangle:50x1" -negate horizontal_lines.png

If using ImageMagick v6, replace magick with convert.

How the Command Works

  1. -threshold 60%: Binarizes the document into pure black and white. Adjust this percentage depending on paper discoloration or scan brightness to ensure lines and text are crisp.
  2. -negate: Inverts the image colors. ImageMagick's morphological operations treat white pixels as foreground and black pixels as background. Negation ensures the black ink becomes the active foreground target.
  3. -morphology Open "Rectangle:50x1": Executes an opening operation (an erosion followed by a dilation).
    • The kernel Rectangle:50x1 is 50 pixels wide and 1 pixel high.
    • Any feature narrower than 50 continuous horizontal pixels—such as character strokes, the crossbars of "t" or "H", dots, and vertical borders—is erased during erosion.
    • Only continuous horizontal lines equal to or wider than 50 pixels survive and are restored to their original width during dilation.
  4. -negate: Flips the colors back so that the extracted lines are black on a white background.

Fine-Tuning Kernel Dimensions

The width defined in the kernel (50x1) controls the sensitivity:

  • If portions of text are still appearing: Increase the width (e.g., Rectangle:80x1 or Rectangle:100x1). This ensures wide letters (like "w" or "m") or consecutive touching characters are completely discarded.
  • If form lines have gaps or breaks: If poor scan quality causes broken ruling lines, use a smaller width (e.g., Rectangle:30x1), or close minor gaps prior to extraction by chaining an initial close operation: -morphology Close "Rectangle:5x1".
