Generate Salt and Pepper Noise in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to add impulse noise—commonly referred to as salt-and-pepper noise—to images using ImageMagick. You will learn the exact command-line syntax for both ImageMagick v6 and v7, how to control the noise intensity using the attenuation parameter, and how to create pure monochrome salt-and-pepper noise versus multi-colored noise artifacts.

Basic Syntax for Impulse Noise

In ImageMagick, impulse noise is applied using the +noise Impulse operator.

ImageMagick 7 (Current):

magick input.jpg +noise Impulse output.jpg

ImageMagick 6 (Legacy):

convert input.jpg +noise Impulse output.jpg

Controlling Noise Density with -attenuate

By default, +noise Impulse applies a standard amount of noise. To increase or decrease the density of the salt-and-pepper effect, use the -attenuate setting before the noise operator. A higher value generates more noise, while a lower value generates less.

magick input.jpg -attenuate 0.5 +noise Impulse output.jpg

Common attenuation values:

  • 0.1 to 0.3: Light, subtle speckling.
  • 0.5 to 1.0: Moderate, clearly visible salt-and-pepper degradation.
  • 1.5 and above: Heavy noise coverage.

Generating True Monochrome (Black & White) Salt-and-Pepper

When applied to an RGB image, +noise Impulse modifies individual color channels independently, producing multi-colored pixels instead of classic white ("salt") and black ("pepper") dots.

To force the noise to be strictly black and white on a color image:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray +noise Impulse -colorspace sRGB output.jpg

To retain full underlying image color while ensuring the noise flecks are strictly black and white, generate a separate noise mask and composite it:

magick input.jpg \( +clone -colorspace Gray +noise Impulse -threshold 50% \) -compose Screen -composite output.jpg

Generating Salt-and-Pepper on a Blank Canvas

If you need a standalone noise texture or mask, generate a blank canvas (typically middle gray) and apply the impulse noise directly:

magick -size 500x500 xc:gray -attenuate 0.8 +noise Impulse noise_texture.png
