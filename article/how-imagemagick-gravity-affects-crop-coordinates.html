How ImageMagick Gravity Affects Crop Coordinates

ImageMagick uses the -gravity operator to define the anchor point and origin for image transformations. When performing a crop operation (-crop WIDTHxHEIGHT+X+Y), the specified gravity determines both where the crop box is placed by default and the direction in which the offset coordinates (+X+Y) shift the crop area. This mechanism allows you to extract regions relative to corners, edges, or the center of an image without manually calculating absolute pixel coordinates from the top-left corner.

The Default Origin: NorthWest Gravity

By default, ImageMagick operates with NorthWest gravity. In this mode:

  • The origin point (0, 0) is located at the top-left corner of the image.
  • The crop geometry WIDTHxHEIGHT+X+Y extracts a box starting at X pixels from the left edge and Y pixels from the top edge.
  • Offsets increase moving rightward along the X-axis and downward along the Y-axis:
    • X_start = X
    • Y_start = Y

How Gravity Alters the Origin

Applying -gravity moves the reference anchor point to one of nine standard locations: NorthWest, North, NorthEast, West, Center, East, SouthWest, South, or SouthEast.

When gravity is defined, ImageMagick first calculates where a crop box of size WIDTHxHEIGHT fits relative to that anchor, then applies the +X+Y offset inward or relative to that anchor position.

1. Center Gravity

When -gravity Center is set:

  • With zero offsets (+0+0), the crop rectangle is centered directly over the image.
  • Offsets shift the center of the crop rectangle relative to the center of the canvas.
  • Calculation:
    • X_start = ((Image_Width - Crop_Width) / 2) + X
    • Y_start = ((Image_Height - Crop_Height) / 2) + Y

2. SouthEast Gravity

When -gravity SouthEast is set:

  • The origin is anchored to the bottom-right corner.
  • Offsets move the crop box inward (leftward and upward).
  • Calculation:
    • X_start = Image_Width - Crop_Width - X
    • Y_start = Image_Height - Crop_Height - Y

3. Edge-Centered Gravities (North, South, East, West)

For gravities aligned to an edge:

  • One axis remains centered while the other aligns to the specified boundary.
  • For example, with -gravity North:
    • X_start = ((Image_Width - Crop_Width) / 2) + X
    • Y_start = Y
  • With -gravity West:
    • X_start = X
    • Y_start = ((Image_Height - Crop_Height) / 2) + Y

Practical Examples

Centered Crop

To extract a 400x400 square from the absolute center of an image:

magick input.jpg -gravity Center -crop 400x400+0+0 +repage output.jpg

Inset Crop from Bottom-Right

To extract a 300x200 rectangle positioned 20 pixels from the right edge and 15 pixels from the bottom edge:

magick input.jpg -gravity SouthEast -crop 300x200+20+15 +repage output.jpg

Important Rule on Layer Offsets (+repage)

After cropping with -gravity, ImageMagick preserves the original virtual canvas and coordinate offsets in certain formats (like PNG or TIFF). Adding +repage immediately after -crop resets the canvas coordinates to 0,0, preventing unintended offset issues in downstream image processing.
