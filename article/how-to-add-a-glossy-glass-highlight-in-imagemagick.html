How to Add a Glossy Glass Highlight in ImageMagick

This guide demonstrates how to apply a glossy glass reflection highlight over an image using ImageMagick. By generating a semi-transparent curved or diagonal white overlay and compositing it onto your source image, you can create a realistic glassy sheen effect directly from the command line without manual graphic editing.

The Classic Curved Glass Highlight

The classic curved glass effect mimics light reflecting across the top half of a rounded or flat surface.

Run the following command in your terminal using ImageMagick 7:

magick input.png \( +clone -alpha transparent -fill "rgba(255,255,255,0.35)" -draw "ellipse %[fx:w/2],0 %[fx:w*0.75],%[fx:h*0.5] 0,360" \) -composite glossy_output.png

How it works:

  • input.png: Specifies the base image.
  • \( +clone ... \): Clones the image dimensions into an isolated memory stack.
  • -alpha transparent: Clears the cloned layer so it is fully transparent.
  • -fill "rgba(255,255,255,0.35)": Sets the highlight color to white with 35% opacity.
  • -draw "ellipse ...": Draws an ellipse anchored at the top-center of the image (w/2, 0) spanning across the width and downward across roughly half the height.
  • -composite: Merges the highlight layer on top of the original image.

The Fading Gradient Glass Highlight

For a softer reflection that fades smoothly toward the center of the image, use a vertical or diagonal gradient mask.

magick input.png \( -size %[w]x%[fx:h*0.5] gradient:"rgba(255,255,255,0.5)-rgba(255,255,255,0.0)" \) -gravity north -composite gradient_gloss_output.png

How it works:

  • -size %[w]x%[fx:h*0.5]: Generates a canvas matching the base image's width and covering the top 50% of its height.
  • gradient:"rgba(255,255,255,0.5)-rgba(255,255,255,0.0)": Fills the area with a smooth transition from 50% opacity white down to fully transparent white.
  • -gravity north: Positions the gradient highlight at the top edge of the image.
  • -composite: Combines the gradient overlay with the base image.

Combining a Curved Shape with a Gradient

To achieve a photorealistic badge reflection, combine a curved vector shape with a fading gradient:

magick input.png \
  \( -size %[w]x%[h] xc:none \
     -fill white -draw "ellipse %[fx:w/2],0 %[fx:w*0.8],%[fx:h*0.5] 0,360" \
     \( -size %[w]x%[h] gradient:"rgba(255,255,255,0.6)-rgba(255,255,255,0)" \) \
     -compose In -composite \) \
  -compose Over -composite realistic_gloss_output.png

This method restricts the fading gradient exclusively to the curved ellipse area, preventing hard bottom edges and delivering a polished reflection. Adjust opacity levels (0.3 to 0.6) depending on whether your background image is light or dark.
