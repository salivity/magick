How to Equalize Image Histograms in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to equalize the histogram of an image across all channels using ImageMagick. Histogram equalization is an image processing technique used to adjust contrast by redistributing pixel intensity values across the entire available range. Below, you will find the direct command to perform this operation, an explanation of how it works, and how to prevent unwanted color shifts during the process.

The Histogram Equalization Command

To equalize the histogram of an image across all channels in ImageMagick, use the -equalize operator.

ImageMagick 7 (Current Version)

magick input.jpg -equalize output.jpg

ImageMagick 6 (Legacy Version)

convert input.jpg -equalize output.jpg

How -equalize Works

When you run -equalize, ImageMagick evaluates the distribution of pixel values and stretches them so that the brightness levels span the full range from 0% to 100%.

By default, ImageMagick applies this equalization to every active color channel (Red, Green, and Blue in standard RGB images). This maximizes the local contrast across the entire image.

Preserving Color Balance (Equalizing Luminance Only)

Equalizing RGB channels independently can alter the color balance and cause unnatural tinting. If your goal is to enhance contrast without altering the hue and saturation, convert the image to a color space with a dedicated lightness channel (such as HSL or Lab), equalize only that channel, and convert it back.

Equalizing Lightness in HSL:

magick input.jpg -colorspace HSL -channel L -equalize +channel -colorspace sRGB output.jpg

Equalizing Lightness in Lab:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Lab -channel R -equalize +channel -colorspace sRGB output.jpg

(Note: In the Lab color space within ImageMagick, the R channel corresponds to the L or Lightness component.)

Summary of Key Options

  • -equalize: Applies non-linear histogram equalization across all active channels.
  • -channel <channel>: Targets a specific channel (e.g., Red, Green, Blue, Lightness) for the equalization.
  • +channel: Resets the channel target back to all channels for subsequent operations.
  • -colorspace <type>: Converts the image into a different color representation (such as HSL, Lab, or sRGB) before applying adjustments.
