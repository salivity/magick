ImageMagick 4-Point Perspective Correction Syntax

Perspective correction in ImageMagick allows you to straighten angled or skewed photos by mapping four distorted source corners to four rectangular destination corners. Using the built-in distortion operators, you can precisely define coordinate pairs to remove perspective distortion from documents, signs, and photographs.

Basic Syntax

In ImageMagick (version 7 and later), 4-point perspective correction is executed using the -distort Perspective operator. The general syntax is:

magick input.jpg -distort Perspective 'sx1,sy1 dx1,dy1  sx2,sy2 dx2,dy2  sx3,sy3 dx3,dy3  sx4,sy4 dx4,dy4' output.jpg

For ImageMagick version 6, replace magick with convert.

Understanding the Coordinate Pairs

The argument string requires eight pairs of \((X, Y)\) coordinates, alternating between source points (\(sx, sy\)) and destination points (\(dx, dy\)):

  • \(sx1,sy1\): Top-left corner of the skewed object in the original image.
  • \(dx1,dy1\): Target top-left corner in the output image (typically 0,0).
  • \(sx2,sy2\): Top-right corner of the skewed object.
  • \(dx2,dy2\): Target top-right corner (width,0).
  • \(sx3,sy3\): Bottom-right corner of the skewed object.
  • \(dx3,dy3\): Target bottom-right corner (width,height).
  • \(sx4,sy4\): Bottom-left corner of the skewed object.
  • \(dx4,dy4\): Target bottom-left corner (0,height).

Concrete Example

If you want to extract and flatten a rectangular document from an image to a final resolution of \(800 \times 600\) pixels, locate the four corners of the document in your source image and run:

magick input.jpg -distort Perspective \
  '120,85 0,0 \
   740,110 800,0 \
   700,550 800,600 \
   90,490 0,600' \
  output.jpg

Useful Options

  • Auto-Cropping to Target Boundaries: By default, -distort Perspective attempts to keep the entire original canvas. To automatically crop the output to just the destination bounding box, use the +distort variant:
magick input.jpg +distort Perspective 'sx1,sy1 dx1,dy1 sx2,sy2 dx2,dy2 sx3,sy3 dx3,dy3 sx4,sy4 dx4,dy4' output.jpg
  • Handling Blank Edges: Use -virtual-pixel to control what color or pattern fills areas outside the source bounds (e.g., -virtual-pixel white or -virtual-pixel transparent).
