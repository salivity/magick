ImageMagick Black-Threshold for Pencil Marks

The -black-threshold operator in ImageMagick is a selective tonal adjustment tool used to force dark pixels into pure black while leaving lighter pixels completely unmodified. In scanned artwork or handwriting, faint gray pencil strokes can appear washed out or uneven. By setting a specific luminance limit, the operator clamps dark graphite values to solid black, significantly increasing stroke contrast and line density without flattening the rest of the image into a harsh binary bitmap.

How the Operator Works

The -black-threshold operator evaluates the brightness channel of every pixel in an image against a user-defined threshold value, typically specified as a percentage or an absolute color channel value.

  • Pixels darker than or equal to the threshold are replaced with solid black (#000000).
  • Pixels brighter than the threshold are left completely untouched, retaining their original colors and tonal gradations.

Unlike the standard -threshold command—which turns everything below the threshold black and everything above it white—-black-threshold is a one-sided operation. It only clips the dark end of the dynamic range.

Turning Pencil Marks into Solid Blacks

Scanned graphite rarely registers as true black; instead, it consists of varying shades of dark and mid-tone grays depending on pencil hardness, paper tooth, and scan exposure.

When applied to scanned pencil art, -black-threshold targets the darkest core of the pencil strokes. Once the threshold is set to match the brightness of the stroke:

  1. Eliminates Translucency: The variable gray pixels forming the body of the pencil lines snap directly to zero luminance, giving the strokes the appearance of solid ink.
  2. Preserves Soft Edges: Because pixels lighter than the threshold remain unaffected, the softer, anti-aliased gray pixels along the outer edges of the pencil lines can remain intact, preventing the jagged "staircase" artifacts often caused by global binarization.
  3. Isolates Backgrounds: Paper textures or light smudges that fall above the threshold are not affected by the command, allowing targeted stroke darkening without immediately altering the paper tone.

Usage and Syntax

The operator is executed via the command line, requiring an intensity parameter:

magick input_sketch.png -black-threshold 40% output_inked.png

In this command:

  • Any pixel with a brightness value of 40% or lower is forced to absolute black.
  • Any pixel with a brightness value of 41% or higher remains at its original value.

Finding the correct percentage depends on the pressure used in the original drawing and the scan quality. Setting the threshold percentage too low will leave dark pencil marks gray, while setting it too high will cause mid-tone shading and paper smudges to collapse into solid black blocks.
