ImageMagick Center Crop with Negative Offsets

This guide explains how to perform a center-outward crop in ImageMagick using negative offsets. You will learn the correct geometry syntax for defining negative coordinates, how centering gravity changes the coordinate origin, and how to handle the virtual canvas to ensure your cropped or expanded image renders correctly without canvas distortion.

Understanding Gravity and Offsets

By default, ImageMagick positions the origin +0+0 at the top-left corner (NorthWest). To crop relative to the center of the image, you must define -gravity Center. When center gravity is active, the coordinate system anchors to the center of the image, meaning positive and negative offsets move the crop window relative to this central focal point.

In standard ImageMagick geometry syntax ([width]x[height]{+-}[x]{+-}[y]), offset values determine the translation of the crop window:

  • A positive offset (+X+Y) shifts the crop window to the right and down.
  • A negative offset (-X-Y) shifts the crop window to the left and up.

Shifting the Center Crop with Negative Offsets

If your goal is to crop a specific dimension outward from the center while shifting the selection toward the top-left quadrant, specify negative values for the X and Y offsets:

magick input.jpg -gravity Center -crop 400x400-50-50 +repage output.jpg

In this command:

  • -gravity Center anchors the coordinate origin at the middle of input.jpg.
  • -crop 400x400-50-50 specifies a 400×400 pixel crop window shifted 50 pixels to the left and 50 pixels upward from the center point.
  • +repage removes the virtual canvas metadata, ensuring the output image adopts the new 400×400 dimensions directly.

Cropping Outward Beyond Image Boundaries

When using -crop to expand outward beyond the original dimensions from the center, negative offsets in combination with dimensions larger than the original image boundary can cause the crop box to sample areas outside the source file.

To allow the crop window to extend outward into empty space without clipping to the source borders, define a background color before cropping:

magick input.png -background none -gravity Center -crop 1200x1200-0-0 +repage output.png

If you apply negative offsets that push the target boundaries outside the source frame:

magick input.png -background none -gravity Center -crop 800x800-100-100 +repage output.png

ImageMagick fills the canvas area outside the original image with the designated background (such as transparency or a solid color), positioning the original image relative to the offset specified.

Resetting the Canvas with +repage

Whenever you perform a crop operation—particularly one involving negative offsets or center gravity—ImageMagick preserves the original canvas geometry in formats that support layers (such as PNG, GIF, and TIFF). Failing to reset this information can result in viewers displaying the old canvas size. Always append +repage immediately after the -crop operation to reset the image canvas to the exact boundaries of your cropped result.
