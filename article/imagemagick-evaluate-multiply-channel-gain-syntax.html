ImageMagick Evaluate Multiply Channel Gain Syntax

This guide explains how to use the -evaluate Multiply operator in ImageMagick to adjust and boost color channel gain. It covers the precise command-line syntax required to isolate specific color channels, apply a multiplication factor to increase brightness or intensity, and reset the channel mask to ensure clean image processing across both ImageMagick 6 and 7.

Basic Command Syntax

To modify the gain of a specific color channel using multiplication, select the channel with -channel, apply -evaluate Multiply with your scaling factor, and reset the channel operator using +channel.

For ImageMagick v7:

magick input.png -channel <CHANNEL> -evaluate Multiply <FACTOR> +channel output.png

For ImageMagick v6:

convert input.png -channel <CHANNEL> -evaluate Multiply <FACTOR> +channel output.png

Parameter Breakdown

  • -channel <CHANNEL>: Restricts following operations to the specified channel. Common arguments include:
    • R or Red
    • G or Green
    • B or Blue
    • A or Alpha
    • Combinations such as RG or Red,Blue
  • -evaluate Multiply <FACTOR>: Scales the raw pixel values of the selected channel by the designated value.
    • A factor greater than 1.0 boosts the gain (e.g., 1.2 increases intensity by 20%).
    • A factor between 0.0 and 1.0 reduces the channel gain (e.g., 0.8 darkens the channel by 20%).
    • Percentages can also be used (e.g., 120%).
  • +channel: Clears the active channel mask, resetting ImageMagick's behavior back to the default channel settings. Failing to include this will cause subsequent operators to affect only the previously specified channel.

Practical Examples

Boosting the Red Channel by 50%

To increase the red channel gain by a factor of 1.5:

magick input.jpg -channel R -evaluate Multiply 1.5 +channel output.jpg

Boosting Green and Blue Channels Simultaneously

To apply a 25% gain boost to both the green and blue channels in a single operation:

magick input.jpg -channel GB -evaluate Multiply 1.25 +channel output.jpg

Boosting the Alpha Channel

To increase opacity by scaling alpha values:

magick input.png -channel Alpha -evaluate Multiply 1.5 +channel output.png

Note: In standard Quantum depth builds (such as Q16), pixel values that exceed the maximum limit (e.g., 65535 in 16-bit or 255 in 8-bit) will clip automatically at peak white/full saturation for that channel.
