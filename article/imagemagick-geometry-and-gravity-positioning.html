ImageMagick Geometry and Gravity Positioning

In ImageMagick, overlay placement depends on the relationship between the -gravity and -geometry operators. While -gravity sets the reference anchor point on the base image, -geometry provides the coordinate offsets and optional scaling relative to that anchor. Together, they dictate both the starting alignment of an overlay and the direction in which positional offsets shift the image across the canvas.

The Role of the -gravity Operator

The -gravity operator establishes an anchor point on the base canvas as well as the corresponding anchor point on the overlay image. Standard gravity settings correspond to compass directions and canvas zones:

  • NorthWest, North, NorthEast: Anchors along the top edge.
  • West, Center, East: Anchors along the vertical midpoint.
  • SouthWest, South, SouthEast: Anchors along the bottom edge.

By default, ImageMagick uses NorthWest gravity. When you set a gravity value without specifying any offset coordinates, the overlay aligns directly with that designated zone (for example, -gravity Center places the center of the overlay precisely at the center of the base image).

How -geometry Offsets Interact with Gravity

The -geometry operator typically takes the format [width][xheight][+x+y]. When used during image composition, the +x+y parameters represent horizontal and vertical pixel offsets.

Instead of operating on a fixed global coordinate plane where (0,0) is always the top-left corner, the offsets measure outward from the anchor point established by -gravity. Furthermore, positive offset values generally push the overlay toward the interior of the canvas:

  • NorthWest: A positive +X moves the overlay to the right; a positive +Y moves it down.
  • NorthEast: A positive +X moves the overlay to the left; a positive +Y moves it down.
  • SouthWest: A positive +X moves the overlay to the right; a positive +Y moves it up.
  • SouthEast: A positive +X moves the overlay to the left; a positive +Y moves it up.
  • Center: A positive +X shifts right; a positive +Y shifts down. Negative offsets move in the opposite directions (-X moves left, -Y moves up).
  • North / South: Horizontal offsets (+X) shift right, while +Y shifts toward the center (down for North, up for South).
  • East / West: Vertical offsets (+Y) shift down, while +X shifts toward the center (left for East, right for West).

Sizing and Ordering

If dimensions are supplied in the -geometry string (such as 100x100+10+10), the overlay image is resized to fit those constraints before the gravity alignment and positional offsets are applied.

For the positioning to resolve correctly in ImageMagick commands, -gravity must be declared before or alongside the -geometry and composite operations. If -gravity is defined after -geometry, the offsets default to evaluating from the standard NorthWest origin.
