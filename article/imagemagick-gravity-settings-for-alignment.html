ImageMagick Gravity Settings for Alignment

ImageMagick uses the -gravity setting to establish a reference point or anchor for image operations such as cropping, compositing, text annotation, and drawing. By setting a gravity direction, you dictate where overlays attach, how offsets are calculated, and which portions of an image are prioritized during resizing and cropping operations.

Available Gravity Options

ImageMagick organizes gravity settings primarily around a 9-point compass grid, along with special settings to clear or reset alignment:

  • NorthWest: Top-left corner of the image canvas.
  • North: Top-center edge.
  • NorthEast: Top-right corner.
  • West: Vertical-center on the left edge.
  • Center: The exact geometric center of the canvas.
  • East: Vertical-center on the right edge.
  • SouthWest: Bottom-left corner.
  • South: Bottom-center edge.
  • SouthEast: Bottom-right corner.
  • None (or Undefined): Disables directional gravity, defaulting offset calculations to the absolute origin (top-left, 0,0) without anchor alignment.
  • Forget: A legacy setting that clears previous gravity states without altering coordinate behavior.

How Gravity Influences Common Operations

1. Offsets and Geometry Calculations

When gravity is set, pixel offsets (+X+Y) are measured from the selected anchor point toward the interior of the canvas rather than always from the top-left:

  • With NorthWest, +10+10 shifts content 10 pixels right and 10 pixels down.
  • With SouthEast, +10+10 shifts content 10 pixels left and 10 pixels up.
  • With Center, +0+0 places the object exactly in the middle.

2. Cropping (-crop)

When resizing or cropping an image to a smaller canvas size, -gravity determines which region of the image is preserved:

  • -gravity Center -crop 500x500+0+0 keeps the center 500x500 pixel area and trims the outer edges.
  • -gravity North -crop 500x500+0+0 keeps the top-center portion of the image.

3. Layering and Compositing (-composite)

When combining two images, -gravity sets where the overlay image is positioned relative to the base image:

  • -gravity SouthEast places a watermark or logo in the bottom-right corner.
  • -gravity Center places the overlay directly over the center of the background.

4. Text and Annotations (-annotate)

When writing text on an image, -gravity determines the anchor point of the text bounding box:

  • -gravity South -annotate +0+20 "Sample Text" centers the text horizontally along the bottom, 20 pixels up from the lower edge.
