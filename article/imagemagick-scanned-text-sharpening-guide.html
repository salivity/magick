ImageMagick Scanned Text Sharpening Guide

Enhancing scanned text documents with ImageMagick requires a balance between enhancing letterforms and eliminating background artifacts such as paper texture, bleed-through, and sensor noise. By combining grayscale normalization, dynamic thresholding, and unsharp masking, you can produce crisp, high-contrast text files suitable for archiving, printing, or optical character recognition (OCR).

Recommended Syntax

For modern ImageMagick (v7+), use the following standard command to isolate text, eliminate background noise, and sharpen edges:

magick input_scan.png -colorspace Gray -level 25%,85% -unsharp 0x1.5+1.2+0.05 output.png

For legacy ImageMagick (v6), replace magick with convert:

convert input_scan.png -colorspace Gray -level 25%,85% -unsharp 0x1.5+1.2+0.05 output.png

Parameter Breakdown

  • -colorspace Gray: Removes color variations and chromatic aberrations introduced by the scanner, converting the image to single-channel grayscale.
  • -level <black_point>,<white_point>: Stretches contrast to clean up the page. Setting the black point to 25% forces faint text strokes to become solid black, while setting the white point to 85% pushes off-white background noise, paper tint, and minor bleed-through directly to pure white.
  • -unsharp <radius>x<sigma>+<amount>+<threshold>: Applies an unsharp mask to restore edge definition.
    • radius (0): Lets the system calculate the radius automatically from sigma.
    • sigma (1.5): Defines the blur size used to detect edges.
    • amount (1.2): Controls the strength of the contrast boost along text boundaries.
    • threshold (0.05): Prevents faint remaining background noise from being sharpened.

Advanced Syntax for Uneven Backgrounds

If the scan features uneven lighting, shadows, or heavy paper discoloration, use background division before sharpening:

magick input_scan.png -colorspace Gray ( +clone -blur 0x25 ) -compose Divide -composite -linear-stretch 1%x1% -unsharp 0x1.5+1.0+0.05 output.png
  • +clone -blur 0x25: Creates an internal copy of the image and heavily blurs it to sample only the background illumination.
  • -compose Divide -composite: Divides the original image by the blurred version, normalizing uneven brightness across the entire document into a consistent white background.
  • -linear-stretch 1%x1%: Maximizes contrast by clipping the darkest and lightest 1% of pixels.
