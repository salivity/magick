ImageMagick Specular Highlight Mask Syntax

Generating highlight specular masks in ImageMagick allows digital artists and developers to isolate the brightest reflections or hot spots from an image using thresholding operations. This guide provides the exact command-line syntax required to extract bright specular highlights into binary or grayscale masks, detailing standard luminance thresholding, soft-mask generation, and channel-specific isolation techniques.

Basic Binary Specular Mask

To generate a high-contrast binary mask that isolates only the brightest specular highlights, convert the image to grayscale and apply a standard percentage-based -threshold:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -threshold 90% specular_mask.png
  • -colorspace Gray: Converts the RGB image to standard luminance, ensuring thresholding evaluates perceived brightness accurately.
  • -threshold 90%: Converts all pixels with luminance values above 90% to pure white (255) and all pixels below to pure black (0). Adjust the percentage up or down depending on the intensity of the highlights.

Grayscale Specular Mask (Preserving Falloff)

If you need a mask that preserves the natural gradient and falloff of the specular highlight instead of a hard cutoff, use -black-threshold:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -black-threshold 85% -normalize specular_gradient_mask.png
  • -black-threshold 85%: Forces all pixels below 85% brightness to pure black while preserving the tonal values of pixels brighter than 85%.
  • -normalize: Stretches the remaining highlight range across the full dynamic range (0–100%) to maximize contrast.

Soft-Edged Specular Mask

To prevent harsh aliasing around isolated specular highlights for compositing, combine thresholding with a slight Gaussian blur:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -threshold 90% -blur 0x2 specular_soft_mask.png
  • -blur 0x2: Blurs the binary mask with a radius/sigma of 2 to feather the edges of the highlight areas.

Isolating Highlights from Specific Color Channels

In certain lighting setups, specular highlights are concentrated in a specific color channel. You can extract and threshold a single channel directly:

magick input.jpg -channel R -separate -threshold 92% red_specular_mask.png
  • -channel R -separate: Extracts only the Red channel before applying the threshold. Replace R with G or B as needed.

Inverted Highlight Mask (Occlusion Mask)

To isolate everything except the specular highlights (useful for shadow or diffuse passes), append the -negate operator:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -threshold 90% -negate non_specular_mask.png
