ImageMagick Specular Highlight Mask Thresholding

Generating specular highlight masks in ImageMagick involves isolating the brightest, most reflective areas of an image into a binary or grayscale matte. This article explains the exact command-line syntax required to extract these highlights using luminance evaluation, hard thresholding, smooth falloff ranges, and channel-based methods.

Basic Binary Specular Mask

To create a hard binary mask where all pixels above a specific luminance threshold turn pure white and the rest turn black, convert the image to grayscale and apply the -threshold operator:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -threshold 85% specular_mask.png
  • -colorspace Gray: Converts the source image to a single luminance channel.
  • -threshold 85%: Forces any pixel value above 85% brightness to white (1.0) and all values below to black (0.0). Adjust the percentage depending on the intensity of your highlights.

Soft Specular Mask with Gradient Falloff

For natural-looking compositing, a smooth gradient falloff is often preferred over a hard binary cutoff. Use the -level operator to clip low values while preserving the tonal range of the highlights:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -level 80%,100% specular_mask.png
  • -level 80%,100%: Remaps pixel brightness so that anything below 80% becomes black, while pixels between 80% and 100% retain a smooth linear gradient.

Multi-Threshold (Bounded) Syntax

If you need to clamp both the lower and upper bounds of specular highlights without modifying the internal range linearly, use -black-threshold and -white-threshold:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -black-threshold 80% -white-threshold 98% specular_mask.png
  • -black-threshold 80%: Sets all pixels below 80% intensity to pure black.
  • -white-threshold 98%: Clips extreme overexposed pixels above 98% to pure white.

Maximum Channel Extraction for Colored Highlights

Standard grayscale conversion averages RGB channels, which can weaken saturated, non-white specular highlights. To catch highlights based on the brightest individual color channel:

magick input.jpg -separate -evaluate-sequence max -threshold 85% specular_mask.png
  • -separate -evaluate-sequence max: Isolates the Red, Green, and Blue channels and merges them by taking the maximum value of each pixel across all channels.
  • -threshold 85%: Masks any pixel where at least one color channel exceeds 85% intensity.

Softening and Cleaning the Final Mask

To eliminate single-pixel noise and soften the mask edges for compositing workflows, chain a Gaussian blur after thresholding:

magick input.jpg -colorspace Gray -threshold 85% -gaussian-blur 0x2 specular_mask.png
