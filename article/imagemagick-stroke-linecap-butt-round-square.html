ImageMagick Stroke-Linecap: Butt, Round, Square

In ImageMagick, the -draw 'stroke-linecap' property defines the shape applied to the endpoints of open stroked paths, such as lines and unclosed curves. By selecting butt, round, or square, you dictate whether the line stops abruptly at the endpoint coordinate or extends beyond it with curved or squared geometry. This guide details the visual and structural differences between each linecap style and provides practical usage examples for image processing.

Understanding the Options

The linecap setting directly interacts with stroke-width. Because paths are mathematically defined by precise center coordinates, the linecap configuration controls how the thickness of the stroke is rendered at its boundaries.

1. Butt (stroke-linecap butt)

  • Behavior: The stroke terminates immediately at the path's coordinates with a flat edge perpendicular to the line's direction.
  • Extension: It adds zero length to the stroke beyond the defined start and end coordinates.
  • Best Used For: Accurate technical drawings, connecting line ends flush against other elements, or when you need total control over exact coordinate boundaries without overflow.

2. Round (stroke-linecap round)

  • Behavior: The line ends with a semi-circular cap.
  • Extension: The curve extends beyond the endpoint by a radius equal to half the stroke-width. The diameter of the circle matches the full stroke width.
  • Best Used For: Softer graphic elements, handwritten or painted visual effects, and preventing harsh edges on thick strokes.

3. Square (stroke-linecap square)

  • Behavior: The line ends with a flat edge, similar to butt, but extends beyond the endpoint coordinates.
  • Extension: It extends past the start and end coordinates by a distance equal to half the stroke-width, forming a square block around the endpoint when combined with the stroke's lateral width.
  • Best Used For: Creating visually balanced corners, grid overlays, or matching the visual length of thick overlapping lines without needing to manually calculate coordinate offsets.

Usage in ImageMagick

To set the linecap style via the command line, pass the configuration inside the -draw parameter along with your stroke properties:

magick -size 300x150 xc:white -stroke black -strokewidth 20 \
  -draw "stroke-linecap butt line 50,30 250,30" \
  -draw "stroke-linecap round line 50,75 250,75" \
  -draw "stroke-linecap square line 50,120 250,120" \
  output.png

In this command, even though all three lines start at x-coordinate 50 and end at 250, the round and square lines will visually appear longer than the butt line because their caps extend 10 pixels (half of the 20-pixel stroke width) past the specified endpoints.
