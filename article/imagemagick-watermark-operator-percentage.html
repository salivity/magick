ImageMagick Watermark Operator Percentage

The percentage setting in ImageMagick's watermark operator primarily controls the opacity, blending intensity, and color saturation of an overlay image applied to a base image. By modifying this parameter, users regulate how transparent or prominent a watermark appears, balancing visibility and content protection without completely obscuring the underlying graphic.

How the Watermark Operator Works

In ImageMagick, the watermark operation is typically invoked using the -watermark option during a composite process. The basic syntax is:

-watermark {brightness}[x{saturation}]%

When a single percentage is supplied (for example, -watermark 30%), it dictates the brightness and overall blend level. When two percentages are supplied separated by an "x" (such as -watermark 30x80%), it simultaneously governs both brightness and color saturation.

Brightness (Opacity and Visibility)

The first (or only) percentage value controls the degree of blending between the watermark and the background image:

  • 0%: The watermark is fully transparent, leaving the underlying image completely unchanged.
  • 100%: The watermark is applied at maximum intensity, making it fully visible and rendering the watermark pixels completely opaque according to their design.
  • Intermediate Values (e.g., 15% to 50%): The watermark pixels are partially blended with the underlying image pixels. Lower percentages yield subtle, translucent watermarks suitable for proofing or copyright notices, while higher percentages create prominent, difficult-to-remove stamps.

Saturation (Color Intensity)

When using the dual-parameter syntax ({brightness}x{saturation}%), the second value controls how much color information from the watermark is transferred onto the base image:

  • 0% Saturation: Removes all color from the watermark during application, blending it as a monochrome or grayscale overlay based purely on its luminance.
  • 100% Saturation: Retains the watermark's original color information entirely during the blend.
  • Over 100% Saturation: Artificially enhances the watermark's color intensity against the background.

Watermark vs. Dissolve

While the -dissolve operator simply performs a linear blend between the alpha channels of two images, the -watermark operator calculates special luminance-based offsets. The percentage setting modifies the actual pixel values in a way that creates an embossed or stamped effect, ensuring the watermark remains legible across both light and dark regions of the background.
