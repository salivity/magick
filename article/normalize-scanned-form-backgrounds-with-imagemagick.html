Normalize Scanned Form Backgrounds with ImageMagick

Background color normalization removes uneven lighting, shadows, gray casts, and paper discoloration from scanned documents to create a clean, uniform white background. This guide provides practical ImageMagick commands and techniques to isolate text and line art on scanned forms while standardizing background tones for improved readability and optical character recognition (OCR).

The Division Normalization Method

The most effective way to eliminate uneven lighting and page gradients is the division blending technique. This method creates a heavily blurred clone of the original image to approximate the background illumination, then divides the original image by the blurred version.

magick input.png ( +clone -blur 0x25 ) -compose Divide -composite output.png
  • -blur 0x25: Adjust the second number based on your image resolution. A radius of 20–30 pixels works well for standard 300 DPI scans. It should be large enough to obscure the text while capturing the background lighting gradient.
  • -compose Divide -composite: Divides pixel values of the original by the background layer, turning any color matching the background into pure white (#FFFFFF).

Full Preprocessing Pipeline for Scanned Forms

To produce professional, high-contrast documents ready for archiving or OCR, combine division normalization with grayscale conversion and contrast stretching:

magick input.png \
  -colorspace Gray \
  ( +clone -blur 0x30 ) \
  -compose Divide -composite \
  -linear-stretch 2%x0% \
  normalized_form.png
  1. -colorspace Gray: Removes colored paper tints, yellowing, or scanner sensor noise.
  2. -linear-stretch 2%x0%: Darkens the darkest 2% of pixels to pure black and clips white highlights, making the text crisp without degrading thin form lines.

Alternative: Local Adaptive Thresholding (For Black & White Output)

If you require strict monochrome output (1-bit black and white) rather than grayscale, use Local Adaptive Thresholding (-lat or -auto-threshold):

magick input.png \
  -colorspace Gray \
  -negate \
  -lat 25x25+10% \
  -negate \
  bw_normalized.png
  • -lat WxH+offset: Evaluates pixel brightness relative to a localized window (25x25 pixels). The offset controls sensitivity to avoid noise or faint pencil markings.
  • -negate: Inverts the image colors so that thresholding correctly targets dark text on light backgrounds.

Parameter Tuning Tips

  • Low-Resolution Scans (72–150 DPI): Reduce blur radius to -blur 0x10 or -blur 0x15 to avoid halo effects around large headings.
  • High-Resolution Scans (300–600 DPI): Increase the blur radius to -blur 0x40 or -blur 0x50 to ensure thick text characters do not blend into the background estimation mask.
  • Batch Processing: Use mogrify with the same parameters to process entire directories of scanned forms automatically:
magick mogrify -path ./output -colorspace Gray ( +clone -blur 0x30 ) -compose Divide -composite *.png
