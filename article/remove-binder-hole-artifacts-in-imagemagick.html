Remove Binder Hole Artifacts in ImageMagick

When scanning documents stored in ring binders, black circular shadows or half-moons frequently appear along the page margins. This guide demonstrates how to remove these binder ring hole artifacts using ImageMagick command-line tools, covering quick margin overwriting, cropping and re-bordering, and automated hole-filling techniques for batch processing.

Method 1: White Out the Margin (Simplest Approach)

If your scanned document has a standard margin with no text near the edge, the fastest way to eliminate binder holes is to draw a solid white box over the affected margin area.

magick input.jpg -fill white -draw "rectangle 0,0 150,%[h]" output.jpg
  • 0,0: Top-left coordinate.
  • 150,%[h]: Covers the margin from the left edge up to 150 pixels wide, extending down the entire height (%[h]) of the image. Adjust 150 to match your scan resolution.

Method 2: Crop and Restore Margin

To completely remove irregular punch holes while maintaining the original document dimensions, crop away the damaged edge and re-add a clean white border.

magick input.jpg -gravity East -chop 150x0 -background white -splice 150x0 output.jpg
  • -gravity East -chop 150x0: Cuts 150 pixels off the left side of the image.
  • -background white -splice 150x0: Inserts 150 pixels of pure white space back onto the left side, preserving the original page width.

Method 3: Selective Hole Filling with Morphology

If your document has text close to the margin and you only want to eliminate dark circular shapes without overwriting the entire edge, use morphological operations to detect and fill dark holes.

magick input.jpg \
  \( +clone -negate -threshold 20% -morphology Close Disk:15 -negate \) \
  -compose Lighten -composite output.jpg
  • -morphology Close Disk:15: Identifies dark clusters shaped like circles up to 15 pixels in radius and closes them. Increase the disk radius if your scan is high-resolution (e.g., Disk:30 or Disk:45 for 300+ DPI scans).
  • -compose Lighten -composite: Merges the cleaned mask with the original image, replacing only the dark artifacts with the lighter background.

Batch Processing Multiple Scanned Pages

To process an entire folder of scanned documents automatically, use mogrify with the margin crop-and-splice method:

magick mogrify -format pdf -gravity East -chop 150x0 -background white -splice 150x0 *.jpg

This processes all .jpg files in the directory and outputs clean PDF documents with binder hole artifacts removed.
