Remove Bleed-Through in Scans with ImageMagick

Scanning double-sided documents often produces faint, distracting text from the reverse side showing through the page. ImageMagick can effectively eliminate this bleed-through (or show-through) by normalizing the background lighting and dividing the original scan against an estimated background model, or by applying targeted level adjustments.

Method 1: Background Division (Recommended)

The most effective technique to eliminate bleed-through while keeping text sharp is dividing the original scan by a heavily blurred copy of itself. This technique flattens uneven illumination and wipes out low-contrast bleed-through.

magick input_scan.png ( +clone -blur 0x25 ) -compose Divide -composite -level 5%,95% output_clean.png

Command Breakdown:

  • input_scan.png: The original scanned document.
  • ( +clone -blur 0x25 ): Creates an in-memory copy of the image and applies a heavy Gaussian blur (radius 0, sigma 25) to isolate the background paper tone and erase the foreground text details.
  • -compose Divide -composite: Divides the original image by the blurred background, turning uniform paper areas pure white and neutralizing faint bleed-through.
  • -level 5%,95%: Slightly stretches the black and white levels to increase text contrast and ensure a clean white background.

Method 2: Morphological Opening with Division

If the blur radius in Method 1 is too weak or blurs dark bleed-through into the text, a morphological opening filter can be used to construct a cleaner background representation.

magick input_scan.png ( +clone -morphology Open Disk:15 ) -compose Divide -composite -contrast-stretch 1%x1% output_clean.png

Command Breakdown:

  • -morphology Open Disk:15: Uses a disk-shaped structuring element with a 15-pixel radius to remove dark strokes (the text) while retaining broader paper discoloration and background texture.
  • -contrast-stretch 1%x1%: Automatically enhances contrast by clipping the top and bottom 1% of pixel intensities.

Method 3: Grayscale and Level Adjustment for Black & White Text

For pure text documents where color preservation is unnecessary, converting to grayscale and applying threshold adjustments strips out low-intensity bleed-through directly.

magick input_scan.png -colorspace Gray -threshold 75% output_clean.png

Alternatively, to maintain anti-aliased, smooth edges instead of binary black-and-white pixels:

magick input_scan.png -colorspace Gray -level 20%,85% output_clean.png

Tuning the Values:

  • If bleed-through is still visible, increase the lower percentage (e.g., from 20% to 30%) or decrease the upper threshold.
  • If faint strokes in the primary text start disappearing, lower the blur sigma (e.g., 0x15) or reduce the lower level percentage.
