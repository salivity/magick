Remove Punch Holes from Scans with ImageMagick

Punch-hole artifacts on scanned documents appear as dark circular marks along page margins that interfere with OCR processing and digital archiving. ImageMagick provides several automated command-line methods to detect, isolate, and remove these artifacts by replacing them with the surrounding background color. This guide demonstrates how to eliminate border punch holes using margin cropping, color flood-filling, and connected-components analysis.

Method 1: Blanking Margin Bands

If the scanned document has fixed margins and text does not overlap with the punch-hole area, the fastest solution is drawing a white rectangle over the left margin where punch holes reside:

magick input.png -fill white -draw "rectangle 0,0 80,%[h]" output.png

To clear punch holes appearing on both the left and right borders:

magick input.png -fill white -draw "rectangle 0,0 80,%[h]" -draw "rectangle %[w],0 %[fx:w-80],%[h]" output.png

Adjust the pixel value (80) to match the width of the punch-hole margin in your scan resolution.

Method 2: Flood-Filling Border Holes

When punch holes touch the outer edge of the image canvas, you can flood-fill them from the edge coordinates without affecting the interior text:

magick input.png -fuzz 20% -fill white -draw "color 0,0 floodfill" output.png

If the punch holes are slightly separated from the border, expand the fill target by specifying approximate hole coordinates:

magick input.png -fuzz 25% -fill white \
  -draw "color 40,300 floodfill" \
  -draw "color 40,600 floodfill" \
  -draw "color 40,900 floodfill" output.png

Method 3: Connected-Components Analysis

For dynamic hole detection across varying positions, use ImageMagick's connected-components feature to identify dark circular blobs based on their pixel area and replace them with the white background:

magick input.png \
  \( +clone -threshold 50% -negate \
     -define connected-components:verbose=false \
     -define connected-components:area-threshold=500-4000 \
     -define connected-components:mean-color=true \
     -connected-components 8 \) \
  -compose Lighten -composite output.png
  • -threshold 50% -negate: Converts the document to a binary mask where dark punch holes become bright white blobs.
  • area-threshold=500-4000: Restricts the filter to objects within a specific pixel area size, ensuring small text characters and larger page layouts remain untouched.
  • -compose Lighten -composite: Blends the filtered mask back onto the original document, lightening the punch holes back to white.

Batch Processing Scanned Folders

To process an entire directory of scanned documents at once, execute the cleanup command in a batch loop:

magick mogrify -path ./cleaned_scans -format png -fill white -draw "rectangle 0,0 80,%[h]" *.png
