Remove Show-Through in Scans Using ImageMagick

When scanning two-sided documents on thin paper, ink from the reverse side often bleeds through and degrades readability. ImageMagick solves this issue by isolating background variations from foreground text using background division, level adjustments, and adaptive thresholding. This guide provides the exact ImageMagick syntax needed to eliminate reverse-side show-through and produce clean, high-contrast document scans.

Method 1: Background Division (Recommended)

Background division is the most effective method for cleaning scans with uneven lighting or moderate bleed-through. It creates a blurred duplicate of the image to estimate the background paper color, divides the original by this background, and normalizes the text.

magick input.png ( +clone -blur 0x20 ) -compose Divide -composite -normalize -level 10%,90% output.png

How it works:

  • ( +clone -blur 0x20 ): Creates a copy and blurs it heavily to eliminate sharp text details, capturing only the uneven background and bleed-through shadows.
  • -compose Divide -composite: Divides the original image pixel values by the blurred background, effectively flattening the paper color to pure white.
  • -level 10%,90%: Adjusts the black and white clipping points to make the primary text solid black and remove residual artifacts.

Method 2: High-Pass / Median Filter Normalization

For scans where the show-through is dense or close to the main text, using a median or morphological filter provides sharper edge retention than a simple blur.

magick input.png ( +clone -statistic median 15x15 ) -compose Divide -composite -level 5%,95% -contrast-stretch 0 output.png

How it works:

  • -statistic median 15x15: Removes fine text while keeping broad background tone shifts intact.
  • -contrast-stretch 0: Automatically stretches the contrast range without clipping highlight pixels, ensuring a pure white background.

Method 3: Simple Level Clipping (Fastest for Light Bleed-Through)

If the show-through is faint and the scan has even illumination across the page, a direct level adjustment is the fastest approach.

magick input.png -colorspace Gray -level 15%,85% output.png

Tuning Parameters:

  • Increase the lower percentage (e.g., 20%) to make faint foreground text darker.
  • Decrease the upper percentage (e.g., 80%) to force faint reverse-side ink into pure white background.

Method 4: Pure Black and White Binarization (Two-Color Output)

If the output only needs to be high-contrast monochrome text (1-bit black and white) without grayscale anti-aliasing:

magick input.png -colorspace Gray -negate -lat 25x25+10% -negate output.png

How it works:

  • -lat 25x25+10%: Applies a Local Adaptive Threshold. It calculates the threshold for each pixel based on a 25x25 pixel neighborhood with a 10% offset, effectively discarding faint bleed-through while keeping main stroke outlines intact.
