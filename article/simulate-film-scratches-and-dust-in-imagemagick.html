Simulate Film Scratches and Dust in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to programmatically add realistic vintage scratches and dust particles to an image canvas using ImageMagick. You will learn how to generate procedural noise and scratch masks directly from the command line, as well as how to blend pre-existing grunge texture overlays using different composition modes.

Method 1: Generating Dust Particles Procedurally

You can create dust specks by generating random noise, adjusting the threshold to isolate individual dots, and compositing them over your target image.

Run the following command to generate and overlay white dust particles:

magick input.jpg \
  \( +clone -fill black -colorize 100% \
     +noise Random -virtual-pixel tile \
     -colorspace Gray -threshold 99.8% \) \
  -compose Screen -composite output_dust.jpg
  • +noise Random: Fills an empty canvas with pure noise.
  • -threshold 99.8%: Filters out almost all noise, leaving only tiny, sparse white pixels acting as dust specks.
  • -compose Screen: Blends the white dust onto the original image while keeping black areas transparent.

To create dark dust particles instead, invert the noise and use the Multiply compose method:

magick input.jpg \
  \( +clone -fill white -colorize 100% \
     +noise Random -virtual-pixel tile \
     -colorspace Gray -negate -threshold 0.2% \) \
  -compose Multiply -composite output_dark_dust.jpg

Method 2: Creating Procedural Film Scratches

Vertical scratches can be simulated by generating random noise and stretching it vertically using a directional motion blur.

magick input.jpg \
  \( +clone -fill black -colorize 100% \
     +noise Random -colorspace Gray \
     -threshold 99.9% -motion-blur 0x30+90 \
     -threshold 20% \) \
  -compose Screen -composite output_scratches.jpg
  • -motion-blur 0x30+90: Smears the isolated white noise points along a 90-degree angle (vertical) to simulate long film scratches.
  • -threshold 20%: Re-sharpens the faint blurred streaks so they appear as sharp hairline scratches.

Method 3: Combining Dust and Scratches in a Single Pass

You can combine procedural dust and vertical scratches into a single command:

magick input.jpg \
  \( +clone -fill black -colorize 100% \
     \( +noise Random -colorspace Gray -threshold 99.7% \) \
     \( +noise Random -colorspace Gray -threshold 99.9% -motion-blur 0x50+90 -threshold 30% \) \
     -compose Lighten -composite \) \
  -compose Screen -composite output_vintage.jpg

This generates the dust layer and scratch layer separately, merges them using -compose Lighten, and applies the combined mask over the base photo.

Method 4: Applying Pre-Made Texture Overlays

For the highest level of realism, you can composite a dedicated high-resolution dust and scratch texture over your canvas.

For textures with white scratches on a black background:

magick input.jpg texture_scratches.png -resize "%[fx:u.w]x%[fx:u.h]!" -compose Screen -composite output_textured.jpg

For textures with black scratches on a white background:

magick input.jpg texture_scratches.png -resize "%[fx:u.w]x%[fx:u.h]!" -compose Multiply -composite output_textured.jpg
  • -resize "%[fx:u.w]x%[fx:u.h]!": Automatically scales the texture overlay to match the exact dimensions of the input image before blending.
