Unwarp 360 Panorama to Rectilinear in ImageMagick

This guide explains how to convert an equirectangular 360-degree panorama into a standard, flat rectilinear projection using ImageMagick. By utilizing the built-in distortion algorithms, you can extract undistorted, perspective-corrected views from 360-degree imagery directly from the command line without needing specialized panorama software.

Understanding the Conversion

A full 360-degree panorama is typically stored in an equirectangular format with a 2:1 aspect ratio (representing a \(360^\circ \times 180^\circ\) field of view). A rectilinear projection represents the perspective view seen by a traditional flat-sensor camera, mapping straight lines in the real world to straight lines in the image.

ImageMagick provides the -distort Rectilinear option (as well as reverse mapping techniques) to sample the sphere and project it onto a planar viewing surface.

Basic Command Syntax

For ImageMagick v7, the primary tool for projection conversion is the magick command paired with the -distort operator:

magick input_360.jpg -distort Rectilinear "<FOV> [yaw,pitch,roll]" output_rectilinear.jpg

For ImageMagick v6, replace magick with convert.

Standard Perspective Extraction

To extract a standard forward-facing view with a 90-degree horizontal field of view (FOV):

magick panorama.jpg -distort Rectilinear "90" perspective_view.jpg
  • Field of View (90): Defines the angle of view in degrees. Standard photography lenses generally range from \(60^\circ\) to \(100^\circ\). Setting the FOV too high (e.g., above \(120^\circ\)) will introduce severe edge stretching inherent to rectilinear projections.

Changing the Viewing Angle

You can pan, tilt, or rotate the extracted frame by passing viewing angles along with the field of view:

magick panorama.jpg -distort Rectilinear "90  45,-15,0" angled_view.jpg
  • Yaw (45): Rotates the camera horizontally (left/right) along the horizon in degrees.
  • Pitch (-15): Tilts the camera vertically (up/down). Negative values look downward, while positive values look upward.
  • Roll (0): Rotates the camera around its optical axis (leveling).

Generating a Custom Aspect Ratio

By default, ImageMagick attempts to match the source dimensions. To output a standard 16:9 or 4:3 rectilinear photo, specify canvas dimensions and use virtual pixel handling to prevent edge artifacts:

magick panorama.jpg -virtual-pixel Black -distort Rectilinear "85 0,0,0" -resize 1920x1080^ -gravity center -extent 1920x1080 rectilinear_1080p.jpg
  • -virtual-pixel Black: Prevents edge repeating or wrapping artifacts outside the projection area.
  • -resize and -extent: Resizes and crops the extracted frame to a strict output resolution.

Extracting Cube Faces

To extract individual directional faces (such as a 6-sided cubemap), run multiple passes modifying the yaw and pitch by 90-degree increments:

  • Front: -distort Rectilinear "90 0,0,0"
  • Right: -distort Rectilinear "90 90,0,0"
  • Back: -distort Rectilinear "90 180,0,0"
  • Left: -distort Rectilinear "90 -90,0,0"
  • Top: -distort Rectilinear "90 0,90,0"
  • Bottom: -distort Rectilinear "90 0,-90,0"
